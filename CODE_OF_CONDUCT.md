# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone. Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and help maintain a positive community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using respectful and inclusive language.
- Being considerate of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this Code of Conduct. They may take appropriate and fair action when unacceptable behavior is reported.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and activities where participants are representing or interacting with this project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports should include enough information for the maintainers to understand what happened and respond appropriately.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and take action that is appropriate to the circumstances.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may respond to violations with actions such as:

1. A reminder of the expected standards.
2. A warning about inappropriate behavior.
3. Temporary restriction from project activities.
4. Removal from project spaces in serious or repeated cases.

The response will consider the nature and severity of the behavior.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct was prepared for the Simple Interest Calculator project and is intended to promote a respectful and welcoming open-source community.
